# The 12 Favorite Problems (Richard Feynman / Tiago Forte Framework)

> **Operating Filter**: These 12 open questions serve as an intellectual compass. When capturing notes, evaluating opportunities, or planning projects, filter them through these questions. Discard or park ideas that do not advance one of these inquiries.

1. **Core Career Excellence**: How do I deliver undeniable domain excellence that accelerates my reputation and impact in the next 90–180 days?
2. **Global Mobility / Geographic Leverage**: What is the most direct, high-leverage pathway to achieve my target international or regional relocation goals?
3. **Enterprise Technical Depth**: How can I master my industry's leading technical platforms, architectures, and systems deeper and faster than peers?
4. **Applied Agentic AI**: How do I build deterministic, production-grade agentic AI systems that bridge complex real-world workflows with autonomous execution?
5. **Bias to Action**: How do I systematically eliminate overthinking and sustain an aggressive, disciplined bias toward physical execution every single day?
6. **Cognitive Moat & Articulation**: How do I build an intellectual moat through structured reading, distillation, and effortless executive articulation?
7. **Financial Compounding**: How do I maximize personal net worth through disciplined asset allocation, high savings rate, and intelligent investments?
8. **Peak Vitality & Energy**: How do I sustain peak physical stamina, sleep quality, and mental focus while handling intensive workloads?
9. **Process & Failure Mode Elimination**: What are the universal bottlenecks and process breakdowns in my domain, and how do I solve them programmatically?
10. **Reputation & High-Trust Network**: How do I cultivate a global network of trusted collaborators, mentors, and partners without superficial networking?
11. **Long-Term Balance**: How do I reconcile high-velocity professional ambition with deep personal fulfillment, family life, and peace of mind?
12. **Compounding Knowledge Engine**: How do I transform every project, reading, and problem solved into reusable Intermediate Packets that compound forever?
